# The earlier project's harness: what to carry, adapt, and simplify

> **What this is:** an evidence-first assessment of the personal harness Wes built over roughly 1,000 hours
> on the earlier bug-intelligence project — the memory model, the documentation system, the enforcement
> layer, and the agent discipline — judged for transfer to Insight Miner. For every mechanism: what incident
> or need gave birth to it, what the documents say it caught, what it cost, whether it depends on scale or
> on the fleet, and what evidence outside these documents would change the call.
>
> **Doc-type:** curated review record. **Update policy:** PRUNE-STALE for the transfer calls (a call that is
> settled by new evidence is corrected in place, dated); APPEND-ONLY for the catalogue's adoption-trigger
> rows. **last-verified: 2026-09-13** (revised this date by a steelman pass — see §11 for everything that
> changed, and §5's "Withdrawn after steelman" for the claims removed. Predecessor: 2026-09-13, first pass,
> every citation re-read in the source file; the steelman re-read every citation it disputes and every
> citation added, and the disputed ones were re-opened a third time in this revision).
>
> The `docs/INDEX.md` row for this file is added by the planning session, not here. Until it lands, the
> doc-currency test will flag this file as unrouted; that is expected, not a defect.

## 2. The harness as a system

The earlier project states its own organising principle once, and everything else is an instance of it:

> "capture is reliable by MECHANISM, not by anyone remembering" [an earlier decision record] — architecture guide, line 2728

and its corollary:

> "when a lesson costs something, it is converted into machinery that surfaces or blocks the mistake before
> it can be paid for again. A recurring bug class becomes a lint; a production drift becomes a regression
> test; a decision becomes a numbered register entry; a narratable fact becomes a derive-live tool; a
> session boundary becomes a hook; a repeated procedure becomes a skill." — architecture guide, line 2730

Four layers cooperate. Each solves a problem the others create.

**The memory layer solves the agent's amnesia, and creates a currency problem.** Context survives in four
mechanisms split by *scope and load-timing*, not by topic: a parent router for cross-project rules, a
per-project `CLAUDE.md` for read-order, the per-machine auto-memory (`MEMORY.md` index plus
`feedback_*`/`project_*` files) for operator judgment that exists nowhere else, and the in-repo durable docs.
"the first three are always-loaded, the fourth must be read" (architecture guide, line 2741). That asymmetry is the design:
always-loaded space is the scarce resource, so everything else has to be *reachable* instead. But the moment
a fact can live in four places, it can be true in one and stale in three.

**The documentation layer solves reachability, and creates a volume problem.** Capture routes by SHAPE, not
judgment — decision to a decision record, calibrated number to a calibration register, data bug to the data-bug
register, harness fix to the fix log, deferred work to a backlog, every session's changes to a session
changelog (architecture guide, line 2738) — so an agent never deliberates about
where a fact goes. Two maintenance rules keep the prose from lying: an update policy per doc class
(APPEND-ONLY for logs and registers, PRUNE-STALE for routers and specs), and "a fact changed in one place
must propagate to ALL its mirrors in the same pass (the #1 historical drift bug)" (architecture guide, line 2773). The volume
this produced is the layer's own indictment: ~4,300 docs, ~81% agent-report exhaust (architecture guide, line 253).

**The enforcement layer solves the volume problem by making staleness visible rather than impossible.**
The documentation checker holds ~64 checks that "check but never edit, so staleness cannot hide while doc
edits stay agent-driven" (architecture guide, line 2739), surfaced through a currency command ("is everything
current?") and a drift-sweep command ("is anything slipping?"). Counts are generated, never typed — "a manual number is a lie waiting to happen" (architecture guide, line 2772).
~22 lint ratchets hold structural invariants with shrink-only per-file baselines, which is what lets the
debt be called "known and bounded" instead of "unknown" (architecture guide, line 2747).

**The agent-discipline layer supplies what no mechanism can: disconfirmation.** Panels are the standing
method on high-stakes questions (value-stage learnings, lines 197–201), stacked so a later round is aimed at the earlier round's
conclusion (value-stage learnings, line 206), and made a requirement rather than an occasional extra after a claim citing evidence
that never existed reached the operator twice (value-stage learnings, line 217). The human is the other half, and the mechanism
is named precisely: "the model's characteristic failure is confident-wrongness it cannot self-detect ...
while the human brings domain intuition and a felt sense that something is off" — and
**"The operator's corrections mattered more than the operator's approvals"** (project-journey log, line 184).

### Where the cooperation breaks

Two seams did not hold, and the documents say so.

**Enforcement sat on a clock instead of on the operation.** The project's own last operator-flagged learning
is blunt: "move enforcement to the OPERATION BOUNDARY - event-triggered at the op it protects - not a clock
[a periodic currency command] or a human's memory. The strongest gates in this codebase are already this shape
(embed -> integrity, import -> schema-guard, promote -> issues-freshness); **the weak spots are exactly the
ones guarded only by [that periodic command] or by a script nothing schedules**" (key-learnings log, line 47). The ratchets themselves were
in that weak class: "direct commits bypass the fast gate ... so RED can drift up silently between full runs"
(architecture guide, line 3126, restated architecture guide, line 3266), which project-journey log, line 180 renders in plain words as "the only thing between a
regression and the corpus was someone remembering to run the full suite."

**The guards did not point where value is delivered.** "Only 1 of the 22 guards the card path - the surface
a human actually reads - which is the real imbalance, not the total" (guards record, line 241).

Both are already fixed in Insight Miner by construction: pre-commit runs on every commit, and the guard
ledger's M1a section points post-run invariants at the run row rather than at internals. That is the single
most valuable thing carried so far.

## 11. Steelman pass

**What this was.** After the first pass was written, every recommendation in it was put to a defence: for
each one, the strongest available case for keeping the earlier project's mechanism *as built*, argued from
the documents rather than from principle. **Forty-five recommendations were defended. Thirty-two survived
with their call intact** (nine of those with their reasoning corrected or strengthened in place); **thirteen
were downgraded to uncertain**; **seven specific claims were withdrawn** as factually wrong; and **twenty-two
mechanisms were found missing from the catalogue entirely.** Every citation the defence disputed was re-opened in the source a third time
before this revision, and every citation added was read in place.

### The cross-cutting finding

This is the single most important thing the pass produced, and it is an argument about the assessment as a
whole rather than about any row.

Across the catalogue, the first pass's dominant move was to convert a mechanism into a rule, a practice, an
operating note or a written invariant: the path-keyed re-point ritual became "a written invariant, not
tooling"; `cwd_path_lint` became "a one-line rule (absolute paths only)"; `rolling_window_lint` and
`step_timeout_lint` became "one-line rules"; `sessions.py` became "the practice"; Scout became "the method";
the fleet's orphan BLOCK became "three general lessons." Each is individually defensible. Collectively they
run against the corpus's own governing principle, stated three independent ways:

> "capture is reliable by MECHANISM, not by anyone remembering" — architecture guide, line 2728
>
> "mechanical rails beat remembered discipline" — project-journey log, line 166
>
> "Automating what humans forget is the only way this stays correct across weeks/months" — key-learnings log, line 618

And the corpus supplies the only quantitative measurement of what happens to an instruction an agent is
merely expected to follow: making a grounding step a script rather than an agent choice fixed a skip rate of
**roughly one time in five** (architecture guide, line 2851).

**The corrected filter is cost, not mechanism-versus-rule.** Where the mechanical version is a few lines — a
path-resolution check, a cwd/wrapper postcondition check, one tested windowed-count function, a block on
backgrounding a long op — the documents say build it. Where it is a subsystem, the rule is the right
residue. Four such few-line mechanisms were promoted to day zero in §7 as a direct result.

This matters more for Insight Miner than for the project it came from, for the reason the assessment already
gives elsewhere: this is an agent-built project under mechanical enforcement, and an agent is precisely the
reader for whom "remembered discipline" is not a category that exists.

### What changed, by kind

**Thirteen calls downgraded to uncertain (§6a), with the defence quoted in each row:** the orphan block's
inclusion in the fleet discard; the baseline/vintage call; the drift sweep; the ID-allocator replacement; the
path-keyed harness's "invariant, not tooling"; PreCompact's rf-check subset; the one-page settled-negatives
merge; the three-marker cap; dropping `[ASSERTED]`; `cwd_path_lint` as a one-line rule; the
`spec_impl_gap_lint` symbol-resolution replacement; the 2–3 agent zero-context count; and the
chaos-testing characterisation.

**Seven claims withdrawn outright** — listed with their corrections in §5, "Withdrawn after steelman." Two
of them were factual errors about Insight Miner's own repo rather than about the retrospectives, which is
the more uncomfortable kind: the assessment asserted twice that the project has no grandfathering while 24
suppressions sat in `.ratchets/suppressions.txt`.

**Twenty-two mechanisms added to the catalogue.** The largest single gap was collector-shaped: the
`saturation_policy.py` fetch stop rule and its unbypassability test (architecture guide, lines 504–574) are the most directly
reusable artifacts in the corpus for a Reddit collector and had no row at all. Next largest was the hooks —
`pretooluse_hook.py`, the best-evidenced hook in the corpus with a documented escalation from REMIND to
BLOCK, and `stop_hook.py`, had no rows while the three thinner hooks did. The rest, by section: the
Critical-Operations pre-read table and the layered health-command flags (§3.1); `MANIFEST.md` +
`check_manifest_integrity.py`, `SYSTEM_RIPPLE_MAP.md`, `DATA_EXPLORATION_TRAPS.md` /
`WHERE_THE_TRUTH_LIVES.md`, and `TRIAGE_LEARNINGS_LOG.md` (§3.2); the registered-exception-that-RAISES
pattern with discovery-from-disk, gated rules and the two-layer contract floor, the hand-curated-artefact
template, the wrapper-postcondition test, the uniform-staleness absolute anchor, `detect_field_collapses`,
characterization suites, `tests/_relocated_modules.py`, the register-a-bundle sequencing rule, and the
four-part operational hygiene set that was discarded with the fleet but is not fleet-bound (§3.3); the
human-feedback "packet" loop and the bind-test precheck (§3.4).

**Nine rows kept their call but had their reasoning corrected or strengthened**, without moving. The pattern
worth naming: in several cases the call was right and the *stated evidence* was wrong, which is exactly the
failure class this corpus names its own worst — a claim citing evidence that does not say what it is said to
say, propagating because nobody re-opened the source (value-stage learnings, line 217). Six of the seven withdrawn claims are of
that shape. The operator was not the first to question them, which is the standard value-stage learnings, line 218 sets.

### What the pass did NOT change

The five design rules (§4 items 1–6), the delivery-surface imbalance finding, the enforcement-at-the-
operation-boundary conclusion, the write-safety model, the memory-snapshot gap, and the `caffeinate` +
retry-ladder + idempotent-resume gap all survived unchallenged or were strengthened. So did the two
structural simplifications with the documents' own words behind them: the semantic search arm (the authors'
own "until real use proves it helps", architecture guide, line 2877) and the committed rendered copy (project-journey log, lines 3 and 3370).

The defence also confirmed the assessment's most load-bearing framing choice: the hold-at-22 counsel and the
four-question admission test (guards record, lines 231–239) remain the standing constraint on everything added above. Nine
new day-zero items and thirty-odd new trigger rows are a lot of surface to have produced from a pass whose
own corpus warns that "The reflex of answering every incident with a new lint is itself a failure mode"
(guards record, line 231). Four of the nine are a few lines each; the rest are conventions, not code. **Nothing in §11
should be read as licence to build all of it.**
