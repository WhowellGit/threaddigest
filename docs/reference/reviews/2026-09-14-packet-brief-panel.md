# Panel on the external review packet and its brief (2026-09-14)

**Trigger.** The packet builder, the refute-framed brief, and the claims list landed in commit `fe0f9d8` (guard G52). Before the first packet goes to three outside deep-research providers, Wes asked for an internal panel to refine the packet and the brief, with an adversarial pass. The plan's rule applies: a prompt template is a gate artifact reviewed like code.

**What each seat was given.** The built packet `.build/review-packets/2026-09-14-fe0f9d8/` (packet hash in `MANIFEST.json`, beginning 855aad), read-only, fresh context, a brief following `docs/reference/AGENT_BRIEF.md`, and an output contract asking for findings with evidence, not conclusions. Three seats ran in parallel: a **dry-run reviewer** (in-family, top tier, chosen by Wes for this panel) asked to do the review as a stranger and report what got in the way before its findings; a **critic of the brief** (in-family, Opus) asked to refute the brief and the README; a **separation auditor** (in-family, Opus) asked to find anything that must not leave the machine and to verify the manifest mechanically. Findings were weighed by evidence, never tallied; each was checked against the packet before it changed anything.

## Verdict

The packet as first built would have produced speculation on the Reddit questions and misdirected effort on absences; the revised packet gives a retriever an index, numbered lines, a state map, the input documents, and a bounded output. The auditor's find (the gate's own test carrying what it bans) is the reason a separation seat runs before every first send. Round one is ready to send; confidence high on the packet, medium on how much the outside providers add to the sample above.
